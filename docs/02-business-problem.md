# Business Problem

Content teams often make weekly plans from disconnected inputs: client notes, Google searches, trend dashboards, TikTok observations, SEO tools, creative preferences, and account-manager feedback.

That process is slow, hard to audit, and easy to bias toward whatever source was checked last.

## User Pain Points

| User | Problem |
| --- | --- |
| Strategist | Needs reliable ideas that match client goals, not generic trend lists |
| Creative producer | Needs briefs with hooks, angles, and format guidance |
| Account manager | Needs explainable recommendations for client review |
| Founder/operator | Needs a repeatable system without hiring a full research team |

## Why Compass Exists

Compass reframes trend research as an operating workflow:

1. Capture client context.
2. Collect market signals.
3. Score opportunities.
4. Generate explainable ideas.
5. Approve and organize the weekly plan.
6. Produce stakeholder-ready reports.

## Success Criteria

- Recommendations are client-specific.
- Source gaps are visible.
- Ideas are ranked with a clear rationale.
- AI helps accelerate thinking but does not silently replace review.
- Outputs are practical enough for weekly production.
